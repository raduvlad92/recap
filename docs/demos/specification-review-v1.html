History and references

Keep dated recaps until you delete them or your account. Preserve original citations and material explicitly linked by creators. Changes to interests or recap language affect future recaps. Corrections and unavailable originals stay visible.

Creator-linked references are links with attribution. They do not trigger automatic research across every linked website.

Cost and subscription pricing

At full use, the example components total about $36/month per reader without reuse, or $8 if shared work averages five readers. Reuse is unmeasured. These figures exclude costs such as hosting and support; they are not ceilings or an all-in quote. The subscription price remains open.
