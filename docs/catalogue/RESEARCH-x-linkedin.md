# X and LinkedIn creator research

Checked **21 October 2026**. Research-only delivery: **120 distinct X accounts and 120 distinct LinkedIn profiles**. No collection job, social-platform scraper, authentication request, subscription purchase or metered provider execution was started.

## What was verified

- Each row contains a real inspected source that positively pairs the public creator name with the exact outbound profile URL. A directory/profile login page by itself was never used as affirmative evidence.
- X: 20 official website cross-links and 100 public directory records. LinkedIn: 7 official website cross-links and 113 directory-supported records.
- Descriptions are brief original editorial descriptions of the public content focus. No audience, revenue, posting-frequency, sentiment or current-job-title claims were imported. No bios, posts, contact details or third-party images were copied into the catalogue.
- URL normalization removes tracking queries/trailing slashes and maps twitter.com to x.com without changing the evidenced handle. Names are not used to invent account slugs.
- Public directory support is explicitly labeled `public_directory`; it is weaker than a first-party cross-link and does not establish current posting activity, content availability or scraper reliability.
- `identityKey` is present only where a distinct official creator website directly linked the account. The same evidenced key can merge channels across these files and the video/social assignment. Do not merge unrelated people merely because they share an employer or a name.

## Editorial selection

The X selection combines science and mathematics, consumer technology, travel, food, photography, entrepreneurship, personal finance, climate, biotechnology, education, design, legal commentary, fitness, HR and marketing. The LinkedIn selection covers AI and software alongside leadership, careers, money, healthcare, psychology, branding, sales, education, travel and entrepreneurship. This preserves the founder's general-reader, consultant and marketing-buyer hypotheses. It is an initial representative catalogue, not a claim that these are the globally best or most popular creators.

Most records are English-language. LinkedIn also includes French, German, Spanish and Dutch content; one X marketing record is Italian-language. Language describes the evidenced main public presentation and is not a tested translation capability or exclusive-language guarantee.

The catalogue includes both people and publishing groups. 3Blue1Brown, Kurzgesagt, Minimalist Baker, Fitness Blender and Rick Steves' Europe are groups; Marques Brownlee, Arun Maini and other evidenced presenters remain people. This avoids converting every studio into its founder or inferring channel ownership from a guest appearance.

## Quality boundaries and rejected evidence

- LinkPilot category pages displayed placeholder rows without creator URLs in their inspected HTML, so none were used.
- MyFeedIn exposed exact account IDs but its general ranking alone gave insufficient individual topic context for this selection; those candidate records were not used.
- FeedSpot records explicitly advertising an inactive account were skipped. Obvious name/handle mismatches discovered in broader candidate lists were not selected.
- A few first-party sites timed out or blocked direct requests. These failures were not treated as missing creators, were not counted as account verification, and did not downgrade a separately supported directory record.
- Site links are evidence of account association, not proof of usable posts/transcripts, collection consent, an endorsement of recap or a creator partnership.
- No real social updates, transcripts, comments, reach statistics, sponsor analysis or sample recaps were synthesized for these records. Pages must keep collection in the planned/not-yet-collected state.
