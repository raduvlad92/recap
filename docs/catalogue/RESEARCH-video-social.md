## Identity and publication notes

Publishing groups remain distinct from their presenters. Examples: Linus Tech Tips, Kurzgesagt, Fitness Blender, The Bucket List Family and Minimalist Baker are group records; Dianna Cowern, Marques Brownlee and John Kanell are people. A shared official-domain identity key is present only where the supporting identity evidence was inspected. Domain reuse by a parent studio must not merge all of that studio's brands, shows and presenters; Linus Tech Tips therefore has no parent-domain identity key.

Some official YouTube websites still publish legacy `/user/` or direct channel-name links. Those precise public profile links are retained rather than replacing them with guessed `@handles`. Other records use permanent channel IDs. Both are valid account references for a planned collection adapter, whose URL support still requires testing.

Language is an editorial catalogue hint (English except ScienceEtonnante French and Manual do Mundo Brazilian Portuguese), not detected language for every update. Many accounts publish multilingual material. Descriptions summarize the public content focus without asserting current posting frequency, professional qualifications, awards or health outcomes. Feedspot's biographies and statistics are not reproduced.

Before enabling collection, verify the actual target account, supported formats, source identity, supplier extraction quality and bounded costs. Published pages must say **collection planned**, provide original links and evidence, and avoid fictional collected items or claims of creator partnership. Research/import/publication does not start collection.
