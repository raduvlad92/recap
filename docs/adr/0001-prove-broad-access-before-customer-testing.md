# Prove broad source access before a customer trial

The founder chose to require broad source access before customer testing, because a limited feed-only trial would not test the promised umbrella across platforms. Public creators must not need to join; usable content comes before comments and audience statistics, and scraping services such as Apify and Bright Data are the selected route to investigate. This trades a cheaper early demand test for earlier collection cost and reliability risk; it does not claim access is already verified, authorize paid runs, or approve the full app specification.
