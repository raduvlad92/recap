Schedule an approved independent auth window. Stage `pk_live_` and the corresponding live Worker keys/issuer against the same reviewed release. 2. Update Supabase third-party trust as rehearsed. Activate the checked reader build and both Worker configs together. Run `--accept` on real browser sessions. The owner must reach the pre-cutover workspace and dated history/library. The new acceptance reader must reach its distinct empty workspace. Verify both existing fixtures still resolve their original owners and retain their inventory. Confirm the ordinary reader cannot administer or reach another owner's data. Require a real normal sign-in for each enabled method that the roster depends on. A one-time ticket proves browser session routing and data continuity only. 3. On failure, restore the checked old reader assets, Worker versions/bindings, GitHub variable/build pin and old Supabase issuer trust. Keep reviewed legacy aliases active in linked mode so original sessions resolve their stable owners, then verify old normal sign-in and data readback. If required by the incident review, disable the new issuer and revoke its sessions after old access is restored. Avoid deleting accounts, revoking aliases or restoring data snapshots as part of routine rollback. The database owner keys and records were preserved through both releases.
