The November 25 production record at commit `580694c` reported 112 draft-producing jobs out of 218 claimed (51.4%), covering 93 of 155 videos (60.0%). The 106 stopped jobs included 44 malformed JSON responses, 19 output-capacity stops, 20 unknown charges and 23 other stops. Six other unclaimed jobs were already held for a charge on the same video and are outside the 123-job replay cohort.

## Cost estimate

The historical run settled USD 1.283637962 across 93 videos with at least one draft, or USD 0.0138 per such video. Including USD 2 in unresolved reservations gives USD 0.0353 committed per draft-producing video. That run used a cheaper checker for the bulk pass.

For a video fitting one transcript window, plan **USD 0.02–0.06 per usable video with both drafts**, at 90% yield. The estimate assumes three cheap extraction calls plus two to four Sol checks, at about 2,000–3,500 input and 250–650 billable output tokens per check. The separate long-recap cohort could cost roughly **USD 0.12–0.30 per recap job** with the same checker, given its repeated source windows and up to nine point checks. These are estimated settled charges. Each new call still needs up to USD 0.10 in available headroom before it starts. Retries, longer output, lower yield and charge holds can increase the effective cost. The first paid sample should replace these assumptions with actual charges.
