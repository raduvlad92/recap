## Why six were held

- **4:** all 20 chunks and 60 exact settled requests completed. Merging produced **618 accepted plus 31 review entries**, exceeding **500**. Publication stopped and the editorial baseline remained intact. These are model decisions, not 649 independently approved names.
- **9:** a named product phrase is missing, several named references remain in review, and an ambiguous variant tag is accepted too confidently. Six short labels mixed named and ordinary uses; source-bound context now withholds their timing estimates without another model call. The inventory remains private.
- **14:** an ambiguous descriptive phrase was accepted initially, then rejected on retry instead of being preserved for private review. Neither result handles the uncertainty correctly.
- **16:** paragraph extraction changed the omissions but still lost three named organizations/offices. Those requirements remain in the reference.
- **17:** the stricter retry accepted an uncertain transcription and a generic object word. An earlier paid-response replay has fewer acceptance errors but retains generic private-review noise. Neither passes.
- **19:** a named agreement is accepted in one chunk and rejected in another. The retry also loses a named setting the earlier answer retained and adds generic private-review noise. Neither complete result passes.

Failed outputs, exact paid requests, original references and scores remain saved privately. No failed inventory was published or relabeled Good to permit expansion.

All four short/long retry comparisons remain strict failures; prior requests were also replayed offline under the current host with zero new model calls. No broad prompt replacement is justified.

Unchanged payloads reuse their exact settled responses and original generation namespace. Changed requests have distinct keys. Every attempt shares one finite **US$3.50 / 249-call** session; no financial counter or old hold was reset.
