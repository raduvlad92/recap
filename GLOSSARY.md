# therecap

Language for a service that turns updates from chosen creators and publications into daily understanding.

## Language

**Creator**:
A person or organization publishing material that a reader follows. One creator can publish through several sources.

**Source**:
A particular creator channel, account, newsletter, blog or podcast a reader chooses to follow.
_Avoid_: Platform, post

**Source update**:
An individual post, article, video, newsletter issue or podcast episode published by a source.
_Avoid_: Source

**Source collection**:
A named group of sources a reader can use as a starting point and edit to suit their interests.
_Avoid_: Fixed subscription bundle

**Platform**:
The service or publishing format through which sources publish, such as YouTube, LinkedIn or a podcast feed.

**Daily recap**:
A short daily account of relevant topics across a reader's chosen sources, with expandable supporting detail and links to the originals.
_Avoid_: Feed, full-text archive

**Recap reminder**:
An optional message telling a reader their daily recap is ready and linking to it.
_Avoid_: Full newsletter, second recap

**Topic**:
A subject connecting one or more source updates in a recap.

**Citation**:
A reference showing which original source update supports a statement in a recap.
_Avoid_: Reference to a source's homepage alone

**Creator-linked reference**:
Material explicitly linked by a creator in a source update or its accompanying notes, retained with the creator and update that linked it.
_Avoid_: Independently verified evidence, automatically followed source

**Reading profile**:
The reader's stated interests and priorities used to judge what is relevant to them.

**Relevance**:
The connection between a topic and the interests explicitly chosen in a reader's reading profile.

**All updates**:
The available updates from a reader's chosen sources without filtering by the reading profile.

**Historical timeline**:
A dated sequence of recaps and the developments they describe.

**Interface language**:
The language used for the reader's navigation, settings and product controls.

**Recap language**:
The language in which a reader's daily recap is written, which can differ from the interface and original source languages.

**Brand mention**:
A source's reference to a named brand or product. A mention does not, by itself, establish sponsorship.

**Sponsor**:
A party whose commercial support of a source update is explicitly disclosed in that material.
